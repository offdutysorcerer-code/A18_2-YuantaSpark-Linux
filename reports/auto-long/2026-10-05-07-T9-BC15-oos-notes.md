# T9 B/C CONF within 15s: 2026-10-05 to 10-07 holdout

- Historical read-only evaluation of a previously selected rule from 2026-10-08.
- Universe is **the same fixed 16 tickers**, NOT the human-curated previous-day candidate selection described by the user. There is no actual historical manual activation log, so the study cannot measure the proposed discretionary workflow.
- C hybrid entry and original exit logic unchanged; max 2 trades per symbol/day, estimated fee/tax, no slippage.
- Native SwingUi T9 JS calculation (T6 segmentation + V9 confirmation). CONF available at bar start + 5 seconds; *not* retrospectively at trigger time. B or C only, within preceding 15 seconds.
- Checks: every one of the 49 accepted holdout entries reproduced the B/C CONF when the T9 JS was re-run on the available bars **only up to the candidate entry**. 49/49 prefix consistency checks passed.

**Caveats:** 10/08 is the in-sample research/selection date. 10/05–07 are chronological *earlier*, not forward chronologically out-of-sample; they are **held-out dates** not used to tune B/C15. Financial performance is sensitive to instrument lot size and costs (1,000 shares each, not equal dollars at risk). No order book or price slippage model. Full-day retrospective T9 event export was used for the first replay, followed by candidate-by-candidate causal-prefix verification (49/49).

Largest 10/05 losses with B/C15: 3406 玉晶光 -21,155.66; 3042 晶技 -4,488.80; 8039 台虹 -4,416.90. Some losses occurred despite entry price above prior close; a mere green-vs-prior-close gate cannot ensure trend continuation.

Suggested future validation (not backtest-proven): user-defined previous-day watchlist; user explicit activation timestamp after open; day-specific opening relative volume, previous-close relative strength, pre-activation price path, and industry breadth as **observables**, not fitted post-hoc exclusions. Activate strategy only for explicitly approved ticker/time windows and test activation conditions separately on forward dates.
